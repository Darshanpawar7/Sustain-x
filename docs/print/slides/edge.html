FlowState · Team 10

This is the Process stage. Every 60 seconds the ESP32 compares the litres through both sensors. Under 5% is normal sensor error, 5 to 15% is a warning, and 15% or more is critical: the servo closes the valve and the buzzer sounds, with no cloud involved. At night, between 2 and 5 AM, any steady flow is suspicious. The rules are in flowstate_rules.h and are tested on a PC: 61 checks pass.
