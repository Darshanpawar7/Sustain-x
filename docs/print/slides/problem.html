Hidden leaks are silent: a cracked joint or a running cistern rarely shows on a bill until a lot of water is gone. The US EPA puts an average home’s leaks at more than 9,300 gallons, about 35,000 litres, a year; one tap dripping once a second wastes over 3,000 gallons. NITI Aayog warned in 2018 that India faces the worst water crisis in its history. A single meter cannot tell use from waste; two sensors on the same pipe can.
