FlowState · Team 10

Each question gets one plain sentence and one chart on the dashboard. Usage comes from the rise in the ESP32’s daily total between readings; loss is usage times the 60-second loss percentage. Today is compared with the previous days up to the same time of day, the same 1.5× rule the ESP32 uses. Change the tariff on the page to your local rate.
