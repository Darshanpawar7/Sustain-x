Analysis first: five questions

What FlowState answers

  1. Am I losing water, and how much?
  2. When do I use the most water?
  3. Is today’s use unusual?
  4. Is water flowing at night?
  5. What does it cost?

1

Sense

Two flow sensors, tank level and humidity, every second

2

Ingest

MQTT every 2 s for live data, HTTPS every 10 s for storage

3

Process

On the ESP32: litres, a 60 s leak check, automatic shut-off

4

Store

Supabase (PostgreSQL), every value checked

5

Analyze

The dashboard answers the five questions

FlowState · Team 10

The course asks for analysis first, so we started from the questions a household or hostel warden actually asks, then designed each IoT stage to answer them. Walk down the five stages: what is sensed, how it travels, what the ESP32 decides, where it is stored, and how the dashboard answers.
