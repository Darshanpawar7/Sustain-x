Team 10 · IoT Mini Project

IoT Water Leak Detection and Consumption Analytics Using Dual Flow Sensors

Two flow sensors sit on the same pipe: one where water leaves the tank, one at the tap. Water that leaves the tank but never reaches the tap is a leak. FlowState measures it, raises the alarm, closes the valve, and shows when, how much and at what cost water is used.

  • ~35,000 Lwasted a year by the leaks in a typical homeUS EPA WaterSense
  • 11,000 L+a year from one tap dripping once a secondUS EPA WaterSense
  • 600 millionpeople in India face high to extreme water stressNITI Aayog CWMI, 2018

Hidden leaks are silent: a slow drip or a running cistern rarely shows on a monthly bill until a lot of water is gone. Measuring flow at two points finds them within a minute.

How it works

The architecture, the protocols on every link, the hardware and the rules behind the numbers above.

Protocols on every link

LinkProtocol
Flow sensors → ESP32Digital pulses, counted by interrupts (~450 per litre)
Level sensor → ESP32Analog voltage, 12-bit ADC
DHT22 → ESP32Single-wire digital protocol
DS3231 ↔ ESP32I²C bus
ESP32 → servo valvePWM, 50 Hz
ESP32 ↔ routerWi-Fi (IEEE 802.11 b/g/n, 2.4 GHz)
ESP32 ↔ brokerMQTT 3.1.1 over TLS (port 8883): QoS 0 telemetry, QoS 1 commands, retained status, Last Will
ESP32 → SupabaseHTTPS REST: JSON POST, connection kept alive
ESP32 → time serversNTP, corrects the DS3231
Browser ↔ brokerMQTT over secure WebSocket (port 8884)
Browser ↔ SupabaseHTTPS queries, and Realtime over WebSocket

Each flow signal goes through a resistor divider, because the sensors output 5 V and ESP32 pins take 3.3 V. The servo has its own 5 V supply with a shared ground.

Processing on the ESP32

  • Litres from pulses. The YF-S201 gives about 450 pulses per litre. Pulses are counted with interrupts and turned into L/min every second.
  • Leak check. Every 60 s the litres through sensor 1 and sensor 2 are compared. Loss = (sensor 1 − sensor 2) ÷ sensor 1.
  • Three levels. Under 5% normal, 5–15% warning, 15% or more critical. Critical closes the valve and sounds the buzzer.
  • Night watch, 2–5 AM. Any flow is logged. 5 minutes of flow or 0.5 L raises an alert. 10 minutes closes the valve.
  • Learning. Over 3 days the device learns the normal amount for each hour and then flags use above 1.5 times that.
  • Safe commands. Valve commands over MQTT must carry an HMAC-SHA256 signature made with the valve key, be under 5 minutes old and never be reused.
